Privacy Policy

Ladder Uploader, last updated 26 October 2026

Ladder Uploader is a private tool used by Adriaan Isufi to upload his own videos to his own TikTok account (@theladderrr) as drafts. It is not offered to other users.

What it accesses: after the account owner authorizes it, the tool receives a TikTok access token, the account's open ID and basic profile information (user.info.basic). It uses the video.upload permission only to send videos the owner created to his own TikTok inbox as drafts.

What it stores: the access and refresh tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private build settings, only to keep the upload working. No personal data of other people is collected, sold or shared.

Removal: access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services. On request all stored tokens are deleted.
